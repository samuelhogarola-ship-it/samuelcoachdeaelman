About me

I am Samuel, a German teacher and coach. I teach from lived experience: living, working and growing in Germany, then helping others make that path easier.

My story in short

From Munich in 2014 to teaching German today with real experience, structure and a more human perspective.

The move to Germany

After graduating, I moved to Munich in 2014. I did not learn German only from textbooks; I had to learn it inside real life.

I first worked in practical roles and experienced directly what it means to build language, work and stability from scratch in another country.

Learning, growing and teaching there

Later I worked in education, supporting people with German and with their transition into life and work in Germany. Experience became profession, and profession gave me a very clear view of what learners actually need.

Over time I reached B2 and later C2+ through the Goethe-Institut. At the same time I learned that students move faster when they have clarity, structure and honest feedback.

Teaching now from a more realistic base

Today I teach online and in person with one main aim: to make German genuinely useful for you, not just theoretically correct.

I value clarity, practical work and warmth. I explain patiently, but I always try to make sure your learning has direction and measurable progress.

What matters to me when I teach

I want German to become practical, understandable and usable in real life. Not as a pile of rules, but as a tool for communication, exams, work and movement.

Understanding before performance

I want you to understand what you are doing and why it works.

Practice before filler

We work on material that really helps you in daily life, at work or in exams.

Warmth with standards

I explain clearly and patiently, but I do not let you keep learning in circles.

If this way of learning speaks to you, let's talk

Tell me where you are now and we will see which path makes sense for your German.
