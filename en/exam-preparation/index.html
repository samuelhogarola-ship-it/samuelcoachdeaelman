Which Exam is Right for You?

Not all exams serve the same purpose. Choose according to your goal:

Exam For What Preparation focus
Goethe German universities, international prestige Module format, strategy and realistic practice across all four skills
TELC Work, migration, quick recognition Sprachbausteine, practical communication and time control
FSP Healthcare professionals in Germany Clinical communication, patient history, documentation and professional interaction
ÖSD Austria, Switzerland, EU recognition Official format awareness and level-specific module practice

Goethe: The Most Prestigious Option

Core skills to train

  • Reading: main ideas, detail, purpose and distractor control
  • Listening: prediction, keywords and comprehension under time pressure
  • Writing: structure, register, cohesion and complete task coverage
  • Speaking: interaction, presentation, argument and natural response

12-Week Preparation Roadmap (B1)

  • Weeks 1-2: Diagnosis and structure. Baseline mock exam.
  • Weeks 3-5: Reading + Listening (2h daily)
  • Weeks 6-8: Writing (daily essays)
  • Weeks 9-11: Speaking + full mock exams (1 per week)
  • Week 12: Final review, rest before exam

Most Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

  • ❌ Reading so slowly that you run out of time
  • ❌ Not reviewing answers in reading (reserve 5 min)
  • ❌ Wrong structure in writing task
  • ❌ Speaking unprepared (low score)

TELC: The Fast & Practical Option

TELC vs Goethe: Advantages

  • ✅ Cheaper (€60-200 vs €100-250)
  • ✅ More available (more exam dates annually)
  • ✅ EU-recognized, work and migration
  • ✅ Shorter exam (less total time)

Exam Structure (B1)

  • Reading: 45 minutes
  • Listening: 35 minutes
  • Writing: 30 minutes
  • Speaking: 15 minutes

Recommended Preparation: 8 Weeks

TELC is more straightforward, fewer surprises. With 8 weeks of focused study, most pass.

FSP (Fachsprachenprüfung): For Healthcare Professionals

IMPORTANT: FSP is not an exam like the others. It's a "linguistic competency test specific to healthcare professionals."

Who Needs FSP?

  • ✅ Nurses wanting to work in Germany
  • ✅ Doctors in training or specialist positions
  • ✅ Therapists, dentists, psychologists
  • ✅ Any healthcare professional (not just nursing)

Structure (Single Format)

  • Medical conversation simulation: 15 minutes
  • Clinical case presentation: 5 minutes
  • Written communication: Medical report writing (30 min)

Key Vocabulary (80% of exam)

  • Symptoms and illnesses (Krankheiten)
  • Medications (Medikamente)
  • Procedures (Verfahren)
  • Medical documentation (Dokumentation)

16-Week Roadmap (FSP requires B2)

  • Weeks 1-4: Medical grammar + vocabulary
  • Weeks 5-10: Simulated conversations with professional
  • Weeks 11-14: Medical reports and documentation
  • Weeks 15-16: Full mock exams (2-3)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long to prepare for B1?

8-12 weeks if you already have solid A2. If A1-A2, then 4-6 months.

Which level do most people pass?

B1. A2 is very basic. B2 is more demanding but achievable in 16-20 weeks.

Can I take the exam online?

Goethe & TELC: partially (reading/listening yes, speaking only in test centers). FSP: in-person mandatory.

If I don't pass, can I retake it?

Yes. You can take it in any following session without restrictions. Full cost again.
